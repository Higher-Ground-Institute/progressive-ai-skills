# Interview transcript — thin

> Fixture for skill evaluation. Dana Whitfield, Ashfield County, and Millbrook are invented.
> This transcript is deliberately thin: the candidate answers in platitudes, gives one real
> piece of earned standing almost by accident, and never states a position with reasoning
> behind it. A profile written from this should be mostly `[NOT ASKED]`, open follow-ups, and
> no-position-yet entries.

**Interviewer:** Ana Ruiz, volunteer
**Candidate:** Dana Whitfield, candidate for Ashfield County Board of Commissioners, District 5
**Date:** 2026-08-01
**Length:** 14 minutes (candidate had to leave)

---

**Ana:** Let's start easy. Name as it'll appear on the ballot?

**Dana:** Dana Whitfield. No middle initial.

**Ana:** And how long have you been in the district?

**Dana:** Twenty-two years. Moved here from out of state, but this is home now.

**Ana:** What made you decide to run?

**Dana:** You know, I just felt like nobody was listening to regular people around here. I've
always cared about my community and I think it's time we had someone who actually shows up
and does the work. Somebody who'll fight for working families.

**Ana:** Can you tell me about a specific family?

**Dana:** I mean, everybody. Everybody's feeling it. Groceries, rent, all of it. You talk to
anybody at the gas station and they'll tell you the same thing.

**Ana:** What's your current job?

**Dana:** I manage the parts department at Coleman Equipment out on Route 9. Been there
nine years.

**Ana:** What do you do all day?

**Dana:** Order parts, deal with the counter, deal with the techs. Mostly I'm on the phone
telling farmers when their part's coming in.

**Ana:** What does that job let you see that people outside it don't?

**Dana:** Huh. I guess — okay, so I'll tell you what nobody knows. When a combine goes down in
August, that farm has about a nine-day window before the loss is permanent. And I'm the person
who has to tell them whether the part is coming from Des Moines or from Germany. I've had
grown men cry at that counter. I know exactly which operations in this county are one
breakdown away from selling, because I've watched three of them go.

**Ana:** That's really useful. Anything else like that?

**Dana:** Not really. It's a parts counter.

**Ana:** What do you do outside of work? Boards, church, coaching?

**Dana:** I used to coach girls' softball but not anymore. Kid aged out.

**Ana:** What are the three things you hear most from neighbors?

**Dana:** Taxes, the roads, and the schools. Same as everywhere I'd guess.

**Ana:** On taxes — what would you actually do?

**Dana:** We need to be responsible with people's money. There's waste in that budget, I can
tell you that much.

**Ana:** Which line?

**Dana:** I'd have to look at it. But there's waste. Everybody knows there's waste.

**Ana:** On the roads?

**Dana:** Roads are terrible. Route 9 especially.

**Ana:** Who decides that — county, state?

**Dana:** That's a good question. I think some of it's state? I'd need to find out.

**Ana:** On the schools — there's a bond measure on the ballot. Do you have a position on it
yet, or not yet?

**Dana:** Not yet. I want to read it first. I don't want to say yes or no on ninety-four
million dollars before I've read it.

**Ana:** Good, that's an honest answer. What about housing — the permitting thing?

**Dana:** I don't know anything about that, honestly. First I'm hearing of it.

**Ana:** Water and sewer? There's a rate increase.

**Dana:** No position. I didn't know about that either.

**Ana:** What will your opponent say about you?

**Dana:** That I've never held office. Which is true. I'd say that's the point.

**Ana:** Last thing — what words do you hate hearing politicians say?

**Dana:** "At the end of the day." Drives me nuts.

**Ana:** I have to note you said "fight for working families" about ten minutes ago.

**Dana:** [laughs] Yeah, I heard it come out of my mouth. That's what I mean. You start
running and you start talking like that.

**Ana:** I've got about six more blocks of questions. Can we schedule a second session?

**Dana:** Yeah, I've got to get back. Next Tuesday?
