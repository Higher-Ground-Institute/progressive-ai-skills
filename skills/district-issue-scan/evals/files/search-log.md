## Agenda pages, by governing body

**Ashfield County Board of Commissioners.** Searched `Ashfield County commissioners agenda`.
First result is `https://ashfieldcounty.example.gov/government/agendas`, which is a page of
links with no documents on it. The link labeled "Meeting Calendar" opens
`https://ashfield.legistar.example.com/Calendar.aspx`. Meetings listed back to 2019. There is
a "Legislation" tab on the same host.

**Ashfield County Planning Commission.** Same host, different body:
`https://ashfield.legistar.example.com/Bodies/PC`. Application AC-2026-0331 appears in the
2026-04-14 packet.

**Millbrook Unified School District Board.** Searched `Millbrook Unified school board agenda`.
Lands on `https://xx-millbrook.civicplus.example.com/AgendaCenter`. Agendas and minutes are
posted as PDFs behind a date picker. No API documented anywhere on the site.

**Ashfield Regional Water Authority.** Searched `Ashfield water authority board`. Board packets
are posted as PDF attachments on an ordinary WordPress page at
`https://water.ashfieldcounty.example.gov/board`. No calendar software of any kind. Twelve
months of packets are there; the 2025-08-19 packet is 94 pages.

**Ashfield County Library Board.** PDFs on `https://library.ashfieldcounty.example.gov/board`.
Checked 2024 through 2026 — routine business only.

**Transit authority.** Searched three ways. Ashfield County does not have one. The county
contracts dial-a-ride through Calder County.

## Local coverage searches

**Millbrook Ledger site search,** `https://millbrookledger.example.com/archive`, searched
`permit`, `permitting`, `Commons`, `data center`, `Northgate`, `Measure B`, `water rate`,
`paving`:

- Four stories since January on individual stalled projects, including "Commons project stalls
  again," 2026-03-14. None of the four states a county-wide turnaround figure.
- One story on the Northgate filing, 2026-04-02, about 400 words. Describes square footage and
  jobs. The word "water" does not appear in it.
- Three stories on Measure B, all covering the campaign committees rather than the assessment.
- One story on the January rate increase, reporting the new rate and nothing about what drove
  it.
- Archive is paywalled before 2024.

condition survey, and the public works director posts a quarterly explainer alongside it with
a map and a written prioritization methodology — it is genuinely readable and it answers the
question people ask.

center.

**Applicant's site,** `https://northgatepartners.example.com/ashfield`. Project page with
renderings and a jobs figure. No water figure anywhere on it.
