# Media map — Ashfield County, District 3

> Fixture for skill evaluation. Every outlet, reporter, email address, and URL below is
> invented. Do not treat any of it as a real contact.

## Assessment

Thin but not empty. One weekly paper does real original reporting on the county with a single
general-assignment reporter carrying every beat. A regional daily forty miles away covers
Ashfield only when something burns down. There is one public radio bureau and one statewide
nonprofit newsroom that will take a well-documented tip. The chamber newsletter reaches
business owners and reprints almost anything sent to it.

A campaign here gets maybe four earned placements in a cycle if it works at it. That means
Ballotpedia, the League guide, and the campaign's own source-of-record pages have to carry
more weight than they would in a healthy market.

## News desert

Not a full desert, but one weekly and one radio bureau is thin enough that losing either would
make it one.

- **What is actually left:** the Ledger and the KVAR bureau, both one-reporter operations
- **Nearest outlet that would plausibly cover a race here:** the Calder Valley Tribune, if the
  story involves the shared water table or crosses county lines
- **Regional and statewide outlets:** Valley Public Media's statehouse desk covers state
  legislative races in the region
- **Nonprofit statewide newsrooms:** the state's nonprofit newsroom takes tips on local
  government accountability and is underused by down-ballot campaigns
- **What replaces earned media here:** with at most four plausible placements in a cycle,
  Ballotpedia, the League guide, the chamber newsletter, the LWV forum video, and the
  campaign's own source-of-record pages have to carry the weight
