# Raw outlet notes — Ashfield County, District 3

> Fixture for skill evaluation. Every outlet, reporter, email address, circulation figure, and
> URL below is invented. Do not treat any of it as a real contact.

Collection notes only. Nothing here has been scored or ranked, and the two-week tallies are
raw counts written in the margin while reading. Geography covered: Millbrook, Eastside,
Corbin Township, and the unincorporated eastern half of Ashfield County.

## Calder Valley Tribune

- Daily. Owned by Meridian Media Group — a chain with 41 titles.
- Circulation 38,000 daily, by far the largest paper anyone here reads.
- Masthead reads "Serving Calder, Ashfield, Whitmore and Penn counties since 1911."
- Two weeks read, 2026-06-08 through 2026-06-14 print editions. Nine items ran under the
  "Local" flag: 4 carried a wire tag, 3 matched a press release word for word (pasted a
  sentence from each into a search engine, all three turned up on the issuing agency's own
  site), 2 were staff-written and both were about the Calder County commission.
- Zero stories about any Ashfield County governing body in the two weeks.
- Byline check going back further: Dale Nordhoff, regional government,
  dnordhoff@caldertribune.example.com, @dnordhoff. Last Ashfield-relevant piece was "Four
  counties, one water table," 2026-02-09.
- Letters: 250 words, web form only. Op-eds: 700 words,
  opinion@caldertribune.example.com. Someone at the desk said they rarely run candidates from
  outside Calder County.
- Election policy: nothing published. Not yet asked.
- Endorses in Calder County races only. Hard paywall; older links redirect to the section
  front.
- Source: https://caldertribune.example.com/submit-a-letter, checked 2026-06-20.

## Millbrook Ledger

- Weekly. Independent, family-owned since 1974. Circulation 5,200.
- Covers Millbrook and eastern Ashfield County, which is this district.
- Two weeks read, issues of 2026-06-04 and 2026-06-11: 14 local items, 11 staff-written, 2 of
  those built from county records. No wire copy in either issue.
- Attends the Board of Commissioners meeting. Priya Raman was in the room on 2026-03-12.
- Reporters: Priya Raman, everything — county, schools, business,
  praman@millbrookledger.example.com, @praman_ledger, "Commons project stalls again,"
  2026-03-14. Tom Beecher, editor, writes the editorials,
  tbeecher@millbrookledger.example.com, "Our view: the county owes an explanation," 2026-04-18.
- Letters: 300 words, letters@millbrookledger.example.com, one per writer per month, writer
  must live in the coverage area, name and town published, phone required for verification but
  not published.
- Op-eds: 650 words, to the editor directly, exclusive, about a week's turnaround.
- Election policy, printed in the masthead box every week: no candidate letters and no
  candidate-bylined op-eds in the four issues before an election.
- Runs a candidate questionnaire in early October and endorses. Raman sends it. Campaign not
  contacted yet.
- Soft paywall, three free articles a month. Permanent URLs, archive indexed back to 2024.
- Source: https://millbrookledger.example.com/submissions, checked 2026-06-20.

## KVAR 88.5, Ashfield bureau

- Public radio. Owned by Valley Public Media, a nonprofit. Six-county signal; the Ashfield
  bureau is one reporter.
- Weekly audience not published. Whatever it is, it is smaller than the Tribune's.
- Two weeks monitored: two Ashfield stories, both original, both posted as text with audio and
  a transcript. Roughly one Ashfield story a week over the last three months.
- Ines Coronado, Ashfield bureau, all beats, icoronado@kvar.example.org, @inescoronado,
  "Ashfield's water authority raised rates again. Nobody said why," 2026-01-22.
- No letters, no op-eds, no candidate-authored content of any kind — equal-time obligations.
  Does not endorse. Does run recorded candidate interviews in October.
- No paywall. Stable URLs. The Ledger has picked up KVAR reporting twice this year with credit.
- Source: https://kvar.example.org/about/contact, checked 2026-06-20.

## Ashfield Chamber Weekly

- Email newsletter from the county Chamber of Commerce, about 1,900 subscribers.
- Two weeks read: no original reporting. Chamber notices and submitted items, reprinted as
  sent.
- No reporters. Submissions to news@ashfieldchamber.example.org.
- Publishes candidate submissions from all candidates equally, 400 words.
- No permanent URLs; the archive is the subscriber's own inbox.
- Source: https://ashfieldchamber.example.org/weekly, checked 2026-06-20.
