# Media map — City of Madison and Dane County, Wisconsin

Written as `district-media-map` would write it. A working contact map, not a list of names.
Ranked by relevance and citation value, not circulation.

## Assessment

Madison is close to the best case in the United States for a down-ballot campaign, and should
be read as the ceiling rather than the median. There are at least five outlets producing
original reporting on Madison and Dane County governing bodies, three of them nonprofits, with
named reporters on the specific beats a local campaign cares about: local government, K-12
education, housing and transportation, health, and investigations. Two separate outlets publish
letters and guest columns with clearly stated word limits, and one of them — The Capital Times —
makes endorsements in Madison Common Council races. On top of earned media, the League of Women
Voters of Dane County runs a video interview series with the City of Madison's own municipal
channel, which produces a durable, free, candidate-attributed artifact for anyone in a
contested race.

Three cautions qualify all of that. **First, this map has one entry that is already a
tombstone**: Tone Madison, an independent politics and culture outlet, stopped publishing in
April 2026. Any media list assembled from a 2025 source still contains it, which is a concrete
demonstration of why this file has to be rechecked every thirty days. **Second, the field a
campaign most needs is the one this map could not fill.** No election-period restriction on
candidate letters or candidate op-eds is published on any submission page checked here. That is
the field the skill warns campaigns learn in late October, three weeks too late, and it is
unverified below for every outlet. A human has to call and ask. **Third, the deadline for the
main nonpartisan voter guide has already passed**: LWV Dane County's Candidates' Answers
publication deadline for the November 3, 2026 ballot was June 22, 2026, extended to 9pm on July
14, 2026 ([LWV Dane County](https://www.lwvdanecounty.org/be-a-voter), retrieved 2026-08-04). A
campaign building its media map in August 2026 has missed it by three weeks.

One structural note that matters more than any single row: the offices a first-time local
candidate runs for here — Madison alder, MMSD Board of Education, nonpartisan Dane County
supervisor — are filled at the **April spring election**, not in November. The next is April 6,
2027, primary February 16, 2027 ([City of Madison
Clerk](https://www.cityofmadison.com/clerk/elections-voting), retrieved 2026-08-04). Every
cutoff in this file must be computed against that date, not against November 3, 2026.

**Know Your Candidates is the single highest-value, lowest-cost artifact available in this
district.** Its format is exactly what `canonical-presence` and the citation-mechanics
reference argue for: candidate-attributed, on the record, all candidates for the same office
asked the same questions, published to a stable municipal URL, and linked from VOTE411. It costs
one hour and showing up, and it is not something the campaign has to produce, host, or
maintain. It is also **conditional on being in a competitive race** — the program invites
candidates in contested races. Two operational consequences: contact LWV Dane County the moment
the filing deadline confirms a contest, and do not build a plan that depends on it if the seat
draws one candidate.

**Wisconsin Watch scores 6 on the rubric and is still ranked first.** That is a deliberate
departure and it should be argued, not hidden. The rubric penalizes it twice for things that are
not weaknesses: it takes no candidate copy because it is an investigative newsroom, and its
geographic overlap is statewide because that is its beat. What it has is the property the whole
ranking is trying to proxy for — its reporting is routinely republished by other outlets in this
district, so one story propagates across several of the rows below it. If you prefer the rubric
to the judgment, rank Cap Times and Isthmus first; the practical difference is small, because
the pitch to Wisconsin Watch is a different kind of pitch anyway.

**Why this ordering and not circulation.** See
[`skills/district-media-map/SKILL.md`](../../skills/district-media-map/SKILL.md) and
[`reference/ai-citation-mechanics.md`](../../reference/ai-citation-mechanics.md) §3.1. The
short version: Ahrefs measured branded web mentions at 0.664 correlation with AI Overview
visibility against 0.218 for backlinks
([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)) — correlational, vendor-run,
on commercial brands rather than candidates, so direction rather than proof. Direction is
enough: a nonprofit newsroom whose work three other outlets reprint beats a larger paper whose
local section is unmeasured.
