# Media map — [JURISDICTION]

Written by `district-media-map`. A working contact map, not a list of names. Every row should
be actionable today: who to email, at what address, about what, by when, under what rules.

Ranked by **relevance and citation value**, not circulation. A 4,000-subscriber nonprofit
newsroom that publishes original reporting on the county commission is worth more here than a
regional daily that runs wire copy.

## Assessment

One paragraph: how much genuine local coverage exists in this district, who produces it, and
what a campaign can realistically earn. If the answer is "almost none," say so plainly —
a short, honest media map is a valid output. See `## News desert` below.

## Outlets

### [Outlet name]

- **Type:** nonprofit newsroom / daily / weekly / TV / radio / newsletter / blog
- **Owner:** and whether it is a chain (Gannett, Lee, Alden, Sinclair, Nexstar) or independent
- **Coverage area:** and whether it actually covers this district or just the metro
- **Does it publish original local reporting?** Check the last two weeks of the local section.
  Wire copy and press-release reprints do not count.
- **Citation value:** high / medium / low, with a reason. Nonprofit newsrooms and outlets with
  clean archives and stable URLs rank high.
- **Beat reporters:**

  | Name | Beat | Email | Social | Recent relevant story |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | | | | | |

- **Letters to the editor:** word limit, address, frequency cap, whether the writer must live
  in the coverage area, verification requirements
- **Op-eds / guest columns:** word limit, address, exclusivity rules, typical turnaround,
  whether they take candidate bylines during an election
- **Election policy:** many outlets restrict candidate letters and op-eds in the weeks before
  an election. **Find and record the exact rule and cutoff date.**
- **Candidate questionnaire or endorsement process:** does the outlet run one, when, who runs
  it, has the campaign been contacted
- **Paywall:** hard / soft / none. Affects whether coverage is readable and citable.
- **Source:** where you found the submission rules, with URL and date checked

## News desert

If this district has little or no local press, fill this in instead of padding the outlet
list.

- **What is actually left:** any remaining outlet, however small
- **Nearest outlet that would plausibly cover a race here:** and what would make them
- **Regional and statewide outlets** that cover this district's issues occasionally
- **Nonprofit statewide newsrooms** — most states now have one, and they are usually
  underused by down-ballot campaigns
- **What replaces earned media here:** which owned and borrowed surfaces have to carry more
  weight because earned coverage is not available

A thin media map is not a failed scan. It is a finding, and it should change the campaign's
plan: fewer pitches, more weight on Ballotpedia, Vote411, forums, and the campaign's own
source-of-record pages.
