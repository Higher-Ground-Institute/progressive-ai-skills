## Ranked issues

One section per issue, most salient first. Three to eight issues. If you have fifteen, you
have not ranked them.

### 1. [Issue name]

- **What it is:** two sentences a newcomer would understand
- **Salience evidence:** the specific record showing people here care. Meeting minutes with
  a public-comment count, a contested vote, a rate increase with a dollar figure, a petition,
  repeated local coverage. Cite each with a URL and a date.
- **Who is affected, and how many:** with a number and a source
- **Stage:** decided / pending vote on [date] / stalled / recurring
- **Decision-maker:** which body, and when it next meets
- **Ballot relationship:** on-ballot / context only
- **Who currently speaks on it:** which officials, outlets, and organizations have published a
  position. Link each. **This is the field most scans skip and the one that determines
  whether writing about it is worth the campaign's time.**
- **Quality of what exists:** is there already a good public explanation of this issue? If
  yes, say where. If no, say what is missing.
- **Primary records:** ordinance numbers, budget lines, docket numbers, minutes URLs
